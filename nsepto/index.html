NSE PTO

Everything happening at school, in one place.

For the parents of North Smyrna Elementary.

In review on the App Store and Google Play

School news arrives in six different places at once, and the one thing you needed is always in whichever one you missed. NSE PTO puts the notifications, the calendar, the volunteer sign ups, and the suggestion box in a single app, and keeps the website and the phone in step so they can never tell you two different things.

Visit nsepto.com →
What parents get

One place to look.

  • ▸Every announcement from the PTO in one feed. Pinned items stay at the top and urgent ones are red, so the thing you can't miss is the thing you see first.
  • ▸A school calendar with every event, and on iPhone you can subscribe so school dates land in your own Calendar app alongside everything else in your life.
  • ▸Tell it your child's grade once, from EC through 3rd, and the feed stops showing you what belongs to somebody else's classroom.
  • ▸The home screen is a digest, not a second feed. Anything pinned or urgent, the next three events, the three most recent notifications, and nothing else.
  • ▸Volunteer sign ups live in the app, so claiming a shift takes one tap instead of a reply-all thread.
  • ▸A suggestion box that goes straight to the board, for the times you have an idea but not a meeting to bring it to.
  • ▸Sign in with Apple or Google. No new password to invent and forget.
What the board gets

Post once.

  • ▸Write a notification, tick "also show on the calendar", and it appears in both places with one date between them. There is no second form to fill in and no way for the two to disagree.
  • ▸Publishing means now by default. Scheduling is something you choose on purpose, not something you have to remember to switch off.
  • ▸Manage events, volunteer shifts, and suggestions from the same admin panel, on the phone or on the web.
  • ▸A Front of the Line Fast Pass auction for fundraisers, with bid increments the board sets rather than the developer.
Built to travel

One school today.

NSE PTO is one app, but it was not built as a one school app. The school's name, colors, grade levels, and time zone are data rather than code, so a second school is a new row in a table instead of a second codebase. Think of it like a school uniform. The pattern is cut once and the colors change per school, rather than every school sewing from scratch.

Two colors never change, deliberately. Urgent is always that red and volunteer is always that green, at every school on the platform, because a parent who moves across town should not have to relearn what red means.
