About the studio

Built by someone living it too.

Threefold Studio builds simple, honest apps for real problems. No pressure to chase margins, no features bolted on to justify a subscription. Just useful tools, built honestly, and priced like they were made for regular people, because they were.

The name comes from a simple idea: a cord of three strands isn't easily broken. Everything here is built on that.
