Project Kappa

Working title

Power is not the enemy here. Pride is.

In development

A landscape action RPG for phones. You play a mortal bonded to a dragon, fighting in real time with your own blade and its breath. The brighter you burn, the harder the dark hunts you, and the more you are tempted to believe the light comes from you.

You save only in towns. You lose whatever you are carrying when you fall. And you choose every mission knowing what it will cost you to be wrong.

The bond

Two of you, one fight.

You are not riding the dragon and you are not commanding it. You fight beside it, at the same time, in the same seconds. Your blade has its own reach and timing; the breath has its own. Most fights are won in the space between the two, when you have committed to a swing and the breath has to cover the opening you just made.

Think of it less like a pet and more like a dance partner who weighs four tons. Neither of you can carry the other.

The cost of burning bright

The brighter you burn, the harder the dark hunts you.

Most games make power the reward and leave it at that. Here, power is real and it works, and it also makes you visible. Holding the breath longer does more, and it also builds something in you that the game is counting. Pride is not a stat you avoid by playing badly. It is what accumulates when you play well and keep playing well, and it changes who comes looking for you.

How it plays

Built for one hand on each side of the phone.

  • ◆Landscape, real time, no turns. Full 360 facing, lunging attacks that commit you to the distance you chose, and soft aim so a thumb on glass still lands the hit you meant.
  • ◆Enemies telegraph in cones before they strike. Every death is one you can replay in your head and name the moment you ignored.
  • ◆Five dragon kin and five mortal classes. Match them and you are granted a blessing; mismatch them on purpose and you trade the blessing for versatility. Both are supported, and the pairing is set once, at the Bond.
  • ◆Breath elements counter each other on a wheel, and advancing a breath keeps the advantage its root element had. Power grows without quietly throwing away what you already learned.
  • ◆Six gear slots and an eight tier ladder, from grey up through platinum, with a forge ceiling per piece.
  • ◆Attunements rewrite one of your abilities rather than adding a number to it. Swap one in town and something you already do behaves differently, instead of handing you another button.
What it costs to be wrong

Three rules that do not bend.

Save in towns

Nowhere else. The walk back out is part of the decision, and so is the walk home.

Fall and drop it

Whatever you were carrying stays where you fell. Greed has a price and the price is the haul.

Choose knowing

Every mission tells you what it will cost you to be wrong before you accept it. Nothing here is a surprise. It is a bet.

About the name

Kappa is not the name.

Kappa is what the project is called while it is being built, the way Cub Watch spent a few months being called Beta App. The real name is coming, and this page will change when it does. Nothing is lost in the meantime, old links will keep working.
