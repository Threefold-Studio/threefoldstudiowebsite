Gamma App

Home Maintenance, Simplified.

In development

A home maintenance reminder app for everyday homeowners: what needs doing, when, without a login wall, a subscription, or bloat.

No spam. Just a heads up when this one launches.

What it does

Just the reminders, done well.

No account. No subscription. No trying to be everything at once.

  • Add home items and systems (HVAC, water heater, gutters, smoke detectors, and more) from a short built-in list or your own custom entry
  • Set a maintenance interval per item, like every 6 months or every fall
  • One home screen shows what's due now, due soon, and recently completed
  • Mark a task done and it's logged with the date, building a simple maintenance history over time
  • Push notification reminders, and a calendar view of everything upcoming
  • A short built-in library of common tasks explains why each one matters, in plain language
How it's different

Nothing standing between you and your list.

Most maintenance apps want an account, a monthly fee, or both before they'll show you anything. This one asks for neither.

No account
Just open it

No email, no password, no login wall. Install it and start adding what you need to keep up with.

On your device
Your data stays put

Everything lives on your phone. Nothing is collected, nothing is sold, and there's no server of ours holding your information.

The core app stays free. A Pro option will cover additional features, with details to come. No ads. No data selling. No subscription, ever.
