Cub Watch Privacy Policy

For the Cub Watch app on iOS, Apple Watch, and Android  •  Effective date: August 6, 2026  •  Last updated: October 9, 2026

Cub Watch ("the app," "we," "us") is a feeding, pumping, sleep, and diaper tracker for infants, built by Threefold Studio LLC. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what choices you have. It is written to match what the app's code actually does. Nothing here describes a feature the app does not have.

The short version

Cub Watch works entirely on your own device. There is no account to create, no login, and no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting of any kind. We do not run any servers, so we never see what you log. If you choose to share a household with other caregivers on iPhone, that data syncs through your own iCloud account, not through us. Purchases go through Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details. The sections below spell out each piece for anyone who wants the detail.

Information you enter

The app stores only what you choose to put into it:

  • Baby profiles: a name, a birth date, a sex (used only to pick the matching WHO and CDC growth curves), and an emoji and color you pick to represent them.
  • Tracking entries: feeds, pumping sessions, sleep, diaper changes, burps, and spit ups, with their start and end times, duration, side (left, right, both, or bottle), amount, and any note you type in.
  • Growth measurements: weight, length, and head circumference with the date you measured, and an optional note.
  • Important People: optional contact details you add for a baby's doctor, dentist, vision provider, school, daycare, or specialists, which can include an office name, a contact name, a role or specialty, a phone number, a fax number, and a street address.
  • App preferences: units (oz or mL, lb or kg, in or cm), theme, sound choices, and similar settings.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or anything else about you as a person. If you type personal details into a field such as a caregiver name or a contact note, that text stays with the rest of your data, as described below.

Information the app never touches

  • No camera or photos. The app has no photo or camera features and never requests that permission.
  • No microphone or recording. The app never records audio. The sound library that plays the white noise tracks links the system's audio permission API, so iOS may list a microphone purpose string for the app, but the microphone is never accessed and the permission is never requested.
  • No location. The app never requests or accesses your location.
  • No push notifications. The app does not send notifications and never asks for notification permission.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. There is no ad network, no analytics or tracking library, and no crash reporting service in the app. We do not know how you use the app.
  • No accounts or social login. There is no sign in of any kind. The only identity the app ever touches is your own Apple ID, and only if you turn on Household sharing on iPhone.
  • No lookups. The WHO and CDC growth percentile curves are bundled inside the app. Your baby's measurements are never sent anywhere to calculate a percentile.

Where your data lives

By default, everything you enter is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere unless you actively turn on Household sharing (iPhone only, described below). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored locally.

Features that move data, and where it goes

  • Apple Watch app (iOS only): the watch app talks directly to the phone over Apple's watch connectivity link. Taps on the watch are sent to the phone and logged there, and the phone sends back the baby's name and the current timer. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Lock Screen timer (iOS only): while a feed, pump, or sleep is running, the app shows the baby's name and the elapsed time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is displayed on your device only.
  • CSV import: when you import history from another tracker, you pick a file with the system file picker and the app reads it on your device. Only the entries it recognizes are added to your log. The file itself is not kept or uploaded.
  • PDF export (Pro): the pediatrician report is generated on your device and handed to the system share sheet, so it goes only where you send it. A copy of each export is kept in the app's own documents folder on your device until the app is removed.

Household sharing (optional, Pro, iPhone only)

On iPhone, you can share a household with up to three caregivers in total (for example, a partner, grandparent, or babysitter) so everyone logs to the same record. Household sharing is off until the owner sends an invite, and it is not available on Android because it is built on Apple's iCloud.

If you turn it on:

  • Sharing works through Apple iCloud (CloudKit), using each caregiver's own iCloud account. We do not run our own servers, and we never see, store, or have access to your data. It moves between your device, the devices of the caregivers you invite, and Apple's iCloud infrastructure.
  • What syncs: baby profiles, tracking entries, growth measurements, and the Important People list for the household.
  • What household members can see about each other: each caregiver's iCloud account name is stored in the shared household record and shown on entries as who logged them. The owner's Pro purchase and trial status is also stored in the household record so that every caregiver's device can unlock Pro without a purchase of its own. No payment details are ever part of this.
  • Invites are a link that you choose how to send (for example, through Messages). The app never sends anything to anyone on its own.
  • Any caregiver can leave the household at any time from the Caregivers screen, which removes their access to the shared record. The owner can remove a caregiver, or delete the household entirely, which removes everyone's access and deletes the shared copy in iCloud. In every case, whatever is already saved locally on each person's own phone stays there. Only the shared copy is affected.
  • Apple's own privacy practices govern how iCloud handles data in transit and at rest. See Apple's privacy policy for that layer.

Purchases and the Pro trial

Cub Watch Pro is an optional one time purchase, not a subscription. On iPhone it is processed by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). On Android it is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your payment information. Apple or Google processes the transaction and the app only receives confirmation that the purchase completed. Their privacy policies cover how they handle payment data.

The free seven day trial starts only when you choose to begin it. The date you started it is stored on your device, and on iPhone it is mirrored to your household record in iCloud if you use Household sharing, so caregivers' devices know the trial is active. It is never sent to us.

Children's privacy

Cub Watch is designed for parents and caregivers to track information about their infants. It is not directed at children and is not designed to be used by a child. The app does not knowingly collect any information from a child. The information you enter about your baby (name, birth date, sex, feeding logs, measurements, and so on) is entered by you as part of the app's core function, and it is handled exactly like everything else in the app: stored on your device, or synced only through your own iCloud account if you choose Household sharing.

Your choices and control over your data

  • Local data: everything you have logged is on your device. You can edit or delete individual entries, measurements, babies, and Important People at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Shared household data: if you use Household sharing, you can leave the household (as a caregiver) or delete it (as the owner) at any time from the Caregivers screen. See Household sharing above for what each does.
  • Exported PDFs: you control where each export goes, and you can delete the saved copies by removing the app.
  • No account to delete: because the app has no login or account system, there is no separate "delete my account" step. Deleting your local data and leaving or deleting any shared household covers everything the app stores.

Data retention

Local data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you delete it yourself. Shared household data in iCloud stays until the owner deletes the household or, for an individual caregiver, until they leave or are removed. We retain nothing, because nothing reaches us.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security: passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint, and encryption at rest as provided by iOS or Android. Data synced through iCloud is protected by Apple's iCloud security and encryption. We do not operate any servers, so there is no database of yours on our side to secure or to breach.

App store privacy disclosures

Because the app sends nothing to us, our App Store privacy label and Google Play Data safety form state that we do not collect data. Data that syncs through your own iCloud account is held in your account, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the dates at the top. Because the app has no account system, we have no way to email you about changes, so please check back here occasionally, especially before major app updates. If a future version adds anything new that handles your data (for example, analytics, notifications, or sync on Android), this policy will be updated before that version ships.
