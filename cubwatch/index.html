Cub Watch

Baby feeding and care, day and night.

Built by a mom who needed exactly this at 3 a.m.

A simple companion for breastfeeding and bottle feeding parents, day and night. Track which side, bottle feeds, and diaper changes, with a built in flashlight and one big button that starts and ends each feeding. Glance back any time to see how things went.

Built for the 3 a.m. version of you

Made to be used half asleep.

  • ✦One big button starts a feed, and tapping it again ends it. Pumping has its own button right below, and the two are tracked separately even back to back.
  • ✦Start and stop a session from your Lock Screen or your Home Screen without opening the app, and a live timer keeps running on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • ✦Feeds are logged as left, right, or bottle, and pump sessions as left, right, or both.
  • ✦One tap logs a wet diaper, a dirty one, or both, along with burps and spit ups.
  • ✦Lamp fills the screen with a warm glow and turns the brightness all the way up so you can see during a change, then hands brightness back to your phone the moment you tap to turn it off.
  • ✦Nothing is watching you. If a session is still running after four hours, Cub Watch assumes the timer got forgotten, ends it at that four hour mark, and flags the entry so you can set the real end time whenever you're up for it.
  • ✦White, brown, and pink noise are free, and Pro adds rain, ocean, thunderstorm, heartbeat, and vacuum.Pro
  • ✦A gentle note shows how long it's been since the last feed alongside your baby's own usual feeding pattern, learned from their rhythm instead of a generic every three hours rule.Pro
Built for the 3 p.m. version of you

Made for the wide awake hours too.

  • ✦Keep unlimited baby profiles, each with its own avatar, and switch between them with a tap.
  • ✦Filter your whole log of feeds, pumps, diapers, burps, and spit ups by type, or by today, the last week, the last month, or any date range you pick.
  • ✦Forgot to hit start? Add an entry by hand, and edit or delete anything already logged.
  • ✦Keep your doctor, dentist, vision, school, daycare, and specialists on file, with office name, phone, fax, and address.
  • ✦Switch between imperial and metric, whatever your clinic uses.
  • ✦Weight, length, and head circumference each get their own chart, drawn over the WHO and CDC percentile lines from the 3rd to the 97th, the same curves your pediatrician uses. Add a birth date and sex, and every measurement gets its own percentile too.Pro
  • ✦Trend charts show feeds per period and what share of them still fall between 8pm and 7am, so you can watch night feeds taper off.Pro
  • ✦Export a PDF of daily totals, the full log, your trends, or the growth charts. Filter first and the export matches it, so you can hand over pumping only for the last month.Pro
  • ✦Share the household with up to three caregivers in real time. Entries, baby profiles, growth measurements, and your Important People list all sync, and every entry shows who logged it.Pro

Every Pro feature is free for 7 days, and no payment is needed to start.

The trial doesn't begin when you install, and it never starts on its own. Tap any Pro feature whenever you get curious and you'll be asked whether you want to begin your seven days right then. So if your first month is nothing but tracking feeds at 3 a.m., the trial is still sitting there untouched.

Tracking, history, unlimited babies, the lamp, and the core sounds are free and stay free. Pro adds the charts, the export, caregiver sharing, and ten more theme packs. It's a one time purchase, never a subscription, and it belongs to the household rather than the person, so anyone you invite gets everything without paying again.
