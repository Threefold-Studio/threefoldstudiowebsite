Hungry Monster Puzzle

Feed the right monster. Feed it first.

In development

Baby kaiju are hungry. Tap one out of the toy box and watch it gobble its color off a giant candy picture. Send the wrong one too early and it sits at the counter, still hungry, taking up a stool somebody else is going to need.

The idea

One tap, one monster, one color.

The candy picture is made of colored pieces, and a kaiju can only bite the color it is. Tap a kaiju and it runs to the counter. If its color is on the edge of the picture, it eats until it is full and waddles off. If not, it climbs onto a stool and waits, and the stools run out fast.

Every kaiju has a number for how many pieces it can hold. Send one with room for six at a color with two pieces showing, and it eats two and keeps waiting. The order you tap in is the whole game.

What turns up later

The shop gets stranger.

  • ●Chonky kaiju that take two stools, and sleepy ones you have to tap twice.
  • ●Impatient kaiju with a clock on their belly. Keep one hungry too long and the level is lost.
  • ●Mystery kaiju in gray who only show their color once a neighbor leaves, and chameleons whose color keeps changing until you tap them.
  • ●Wrapped candy that takes two bites, jawbreakers nobody can eat, striped candy that counts as two colors, and fizz candy that pops its neighbors.
  • ●Sugar Rush days against the clock, and ice cream that melts if you leave it too long.
How much of it there is

Five hundred levels at launch.

Each level is a new candy picture, and the pictures fill a Candy Album as you clear them. Every species of kaiju you meet goes in the Monster Book. A daily challenge, a Sweet Pass season, and a toy city skyline that lights up one candy shop at a time.

Still being built.

Kaiju Kandy is in testing. There is no release date yet, and there will not be one here until it is a date worth holding to.
