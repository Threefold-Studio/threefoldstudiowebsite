Delta App

Trip planning, built for your whole crew.

In development

A group trip planning app built around persistent groups, not just one-off trips. Family, friends, or any recurring travel crew can each have their own standing group, with full trip history saved across every trip you've taken together.

Back to Threefold Studio
What makes it different

Your crew, remembered.

Anyone can join a single trip for free via an invite link, no account needed. Standing groups with saved history are for the crews who travel together again and again.

Plans

Free to join. Pro to remember.

Free
Any single trip
  • Full access via invite link, no account required
  • Real-time collaborative editing with your group
  • Dates, times, lodging, activities, attendee list
Pro
Every trip, every group
  • Persistent named groups across every trip you take
  • Full trip history, saved forever
  • Belong to multiple groups at once
  • Shared photo albums and exportable trip summaries
  • Custom categories beyond the built-in defaults

Pro will be offered as a yearly plan or a one time lifetime purchase. We'll share the numbers closer to launch.
