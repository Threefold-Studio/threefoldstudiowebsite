Island Pickup Privacy Policy

For the Island Pickup app on iOS and Android  •  Effective date: November 1, 2026  •  Last updated: November 1, 2026

Island Pickup ("the app," "we," "us") is a puzzle game built by Threefold Studio LLC. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what choices you have. It is written to match what the app's code actually does. Nothing here describes a feature the app does not have.

The short version

Island Pickup saves your progress to the cloud so it survives a new phone. To do that, the app creates an anonymous player account the first time it runs, with no name, email, or sign in required. You can attach that save to your Apple or Google account if you want it back after a reinstall. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and you can decline ad personalization. Optional purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, and we never see your payment details. We never sell your information.

Your player account

On first launch the app asks our backend, Supabase, for an anonymous account. It has a random id and nothing else. Everything below is stored against that id:

  • Progress: your current level, coins, boosters, lives and when they last refilled, which features you have seen, and your settings (sound, haptics, reminders).
  • Season pass progress: which rewards you have claimed in the current season.
  • Level results: for each level you play, whether you won, how it ended, how many moves it took, how long it took, and how many boosters you used. We use these to tune level difficulty. They are not shown to other players.
  • Device basics: whether you are on iOS or Android, the app version, and when the app was last opened, so we know which versions are in use.

Signing in (optional)

You can link your save to an Apple or Google account from the Cloud Save screen. This is never required to play. If you do, Apple or Google sends us the identifier for your account and, depending on what you allow on their consent screen, your name and email address. We use these only to recognize you on a new device or after a reinstall. We do not email you.

Ads

Island Pickup shows ads through Google AdMob: reward ads that you choose to watch for in-game bonuses, and short ads between some levels. Reward ads never play on their own. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising identifier and basic device and usage information, such as device model, operating system, IP address, and how you interact with the ads. Google's privacy policy governs that data. See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where US state privacy laws apply, Google's consent form appears before any ad loads, and your answer controls whether ads are personalized.
  • On iPhone, the app asks once whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you say no, AdMob still shows ads, but they are not personalized to you.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels. Reward ads stay available as an option.

The game plays exactly the same whichever way you answer.

In-app purchases

Island Pickup offers optional purchases: coin packs, bundles, Remove Ads, and a season pass. They are sold through the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles payment, and we never see card or payment details. Purchases are processed by RevenueCat, which receives your anonymous player id and the purchase record so your purchases can be restored on a new phone. We store a record of each purchase (product, store, transaction id, and price) with your player account. Coins and boosters can still be earned by playing or from reward ads.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules a reminder when your lives are full and one daily reminder on your phone. No server is involved, and they are off until you turn them on. This version of the app does not send push notifications from us.

Information the app does not touch

  • No analytics or crash reporting services. Apart from what the AdMob SDK collects to serve ads, the level results above are the only usage data we keep, and they go to our own database.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The app never requests them. It reads your phone's time zone only to draw the island sky at the right time of day; the time zone is not sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Island Pickup is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not ask for a name, age, or email, and the anonymous account holds nothing that identifies a person. If you believe a child under 13 has linked an Apple or Google account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and control over your data

  • Play without signing in: the default. Your save is tied only to the anonymous id on your phone.
  • Ad personalization: decline tracking when iOS asks, or change it later under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Google settings.
  • Delete your account: the Cloud Save screen has a Delete account option. It removes your player account and every row tied to it, including progress, level results, purchase records stored on our servers, and any linked Apple or Google identity. This cannot be undone. It does not remove data that Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or AdMob already hold under their own policies; use their controls for that.
  • Deleting the app: removes the local copy of your progress. The cloud copy stays until you delete the account, so you can come back to it.

Data retention

Your player account and everything tied to it are kept until you delete the account. Level results are kept to tune difficulty and may be retained in aggregate form, without any player id, after an account is deleted.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase: hosts the database and the sign-in system that the player account lives in.
  • Google AdMob: serves the ads, with Google's User Messaging Platform for the consent form.
  • RevenueCat: processes in-app purchases and keeps the record that lets you restore them.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, if you choose to link an account.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its typeface from Google's font servers the first time it runs and caches it on the device.

Each of these providers processes data on our behalf under its own privacy policy. AdMob is the exception: Google serves ads as an independent business and its privacy policy, linked above, governs what it does with ad data.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security. Data in our database is protected by row-level rules so that one player can only read and write their own rows, and connections to it are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the dates at the top. Anything new that handles your information, such as push notifications, will be described here before the version that adds it ships.
