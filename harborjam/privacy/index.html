Harbor Jam Privacy Policy

For the Harbor Jam app on iOS and Android  •  Effective date: November 1, 2026  •  Last updated: November 1, 2026

Harbor Jam ("the app," "we," "us") is a boat sorting puzzle game built by Threefold Studio LLC. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what choices you have. It is written to match what the app's code actually does. Nothing here describes a feature the app does not have.

The short version

Harbor Jam saves your progress to the cloud so it survives a new phone. To do that, the app creates an anonymous player account the first time it runs, with no name, email, or sign in required. You can attach that save to your Apple or Google account if you want it back after a reinstall. The app has no advertising and no in-app purchases in this version. We never sell your information.

Your player account

On first launch the app asks our backend, Supabase, for an anonymous account. It has a random id and nothing else. Everything below is stored against that id:

  • Progress: your current level, coins, boosters, lives and when they last refilled, which features you have seen, and your settings (sound, haptics, reminders).
  • Season pass progress: which rewards you have claimed in the current season.
  • Level results: for each level you play, whether you won, how it ended, how many moves it took, how long it took, and how many boosters you used. We use these to tune level difficulty. They are not shown to other players.
  • Device basics: whether you are on iOS or Android, the app version, and when the app was last opened, so we know which versions are in use.

Signing in (optional)

You can link your save to an Apple or Google account from the Cloud Save screen. This is never required to play. If you do, Apple or Google sends us the identifier for your account and, depending on what you allow on their consent screen, your name and email address. We use these only to recognize you on a new device or after a reinstall. We do not email you.

Notifications

  • Reminders on your device: if you turn reminders on, the app schedules a reminder when your lives are full and one daily reminder on your phone. No server is involved, and they are off until you turn them on.
  • Messages from us: if you allow notifications, your phone gets a push token from Apple or Google. The app saves that token with your player account and subscribes to a general topic so we can send news about the game, such as a new season or a new set of levels. Pushes go through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Turning notifications off in your phone's settings stops them.

Information the app does not touch

  • No advertising. This version of Harbor Jam has no ad network in it.
  • No purchases. This version has no in-app purchases. Coins and boosters are earned in the game.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services. The level results above are the only usage data we keep, and they go to our own database, not to a third party.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The app never requests them. It reads your phone's time zone only to draw the harbor sky at the right time of day; the time zone is not sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Harbor Jam is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not ask for a name, age, or email, and the anonymous account holds nothing that identifies a person. If you believe a child under 13 has linked an Apple or Google account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and control over your data

  • Play without signing in: the default. Your save is tied only to the anonymous id on your phone.
  • Delete your account: the Cloud Save screen has a Delete account option. It removes your player account and every row tied to it, including progress, level results, push tokens, and any linked Apple or Google identity. This cannot be undone.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app and pushes off in your phone's settings.
  • Deleting the app: removes the local copy of your progress. The cloud copy stays until you delete the account, so you can come back to it.

Data retention

Your player account and everything tied to it are kept until you delete the account. Level results are kept to tune difficulty and may be retained in aggregate form, without any player id, after an account is deleted.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase: hosts the database and the sign-in system that the player account lives in.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, if you choose to link an account.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its typeface from Google's font servers the first time it runs and caches it on the device.

Each of these providers processes data on our behalf under its own privacy policy, and none of them is allowed to use it for anything else.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security. Data in our database is protected by row-level rules so that one player can only read and write their own rows, and connections to it are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the dates at the top. Anything new that handles your information, such as advertising or in-app purchases, will be described here before the version that adds it ships.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how Harbor Jam handles information, contact us at hello@threefoldstudio.co.
