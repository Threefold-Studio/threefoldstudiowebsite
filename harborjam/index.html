A one thumb boat puzzle.

Every boat is in somebody's way.

In development

The marina is jammed. Tap a boat and it sails, but only if the lane ahead of it is clear, and the boat blocking that lane is itself blocked by another. Load the right colors onto the right ships, get everybody out, and the harbor empties.

The idea

One thumb, one hand, one harbor.

Harbor Jam is built for the hand you actually have free. Portrait, one thumb, no drag and no dexterity. Every move is a single tap on a boat, and the only question is which boat, and in what order.

Think of it like the car park puzzle where you have to get one vehicle out, except the cars have cargo, the exit is a ship that only takes matching colors, and the ship leaves when it is full.

What is in the water

Not every boat behaves.

  • ●Tugboats tow a line of boats behind them, in colors that are not their own, and will not sail at all until a ship of their own color is docked.
  • ●Submarines dive under the boat in front and surface in the next gap, so they need the gap to exist.
  • ●Sailboats wait for the wind. Fishing boats leave a buoy behind them. Low bridges let the small hulls through and stop the big ones.
  • ●Whirlpools bend a route sideways, and partway through the game they start turning the other way.
  • ●Freighters that take two colors at once, express freighters that rotate to the back of the queue, and cargo marked by symbol instead of color.
  • ●Pirates, eventually. A cannon that blocks a berth, and a gunboat that clears a lane.
How much of it there is

Four hundred levels, all of them winnable.

400hand tuned levels, each one retuned every time a rule changed
100%proven solvable by a solver, with four slots and no boosters
7cargo colors, kept far enough apart to tell at a glance

That second number is the one worth explaining. Every level is run through a solver that has to finish it using only what a player starts with, no purchases and no help. A level that cannot be beaten honestly does not ship. It is checked again, all four hundred of them, every time a rule changes.

Around the game

It remembers you.

  • ●Sign in with Apple or Google and your progress follows you to another phone.
  • ●Daily rewards and streaks, and a harbor you can decorate as you go.
  • ●Captains and cosmetics to earn, none of which change the colors you have to read.

Still being built.
